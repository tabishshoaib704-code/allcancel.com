Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

1. Information We Collect

AllCancel ("we," "us," or "our") collects information you provide directly when using our cancellation services:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Account Information: Service provider account numbers, billing details, and contract information
  • Communication Records: Correspondence between you, our team, and service providers
  • Payment Information: Credit card or payment details processed through secure third-party providers

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process and complete your service cancellation requests
  • Communicate with service providers on your behalf
  • Send you updates about your cancellation status
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With telecom and energy companies to process your cancellation
  • Third-Party Services: With payment processors and essential service providers
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure servers, and access controls to protect your information.
